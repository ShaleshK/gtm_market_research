**Ideal Customer Profiles**
-------------------------

### **Segment 1: Small Businesses and Solopreneurs**

* Description: Small businesses and solopreneurs looking for an intuitive and user-friendly design tool to create professional-looking graphics and branding.
* Pain Points:
	+ Time-consuming to design and create graphics
	+ Limited design expertise
	+ Difficulty integrating with existing website and branding
* Desired Features:
	+ Easy-to-use interface
	+ Access to templates and design assets
	+ In-cost-effective pricing

### **Segment 2: Enterprise and Large-Scale Organizations**

* Description: Large-scale organizations looking for a robust and feature-rich design tool to manage multiple design projects and collaborate with teams.
* Pain Points:
	+ Difficulty managing multiple design projects and teams
	+ Limited design expertise and resources
	+ Difficulty integrating with existing design systems
* Desired Features:
	+ Advanced collaboration and project management features
	+ Access to AI-powered design tools and automation
	+ Scalable pricing for large teams and projects

### **Segment 3: Non-Designers and Small Teams**

* Description: Non-designers and small teams looking for a no-code development and web hosting solution to create professional-looking websites and applications.
* Pain Points:
	+ Limited design and development expertise
	+ Difficulty creating and managing websites and applications
	+ Limited budget and resources
* Desired Features:
	+ Easy-to-use interface for non-designers
	+ No-code development and web hosting
	+ Affordable pricing for small teams and projects

**90-Day Launch Map**
--------------------

### **Weeks 1-4: Market Research and Analysis**

* Objective: Conduct market research and analysis to identify key competitors and market gaps.
* Activities:
	+ Conduct competitor analysis and market research reports.
	+ Identify unique selling points, features, and pricing strategies of key competitors.
	+ Analyze user pain points and needs.

### **Weeks 5-8: Product Development and Iteration**

* Objective: Develop and iterate on the design tool to meet the needs and pain points of target customers.
* Activities:
	+ Development of the design tool, focusing on ease of use and accessibility.
	+ Iteration and testing based on user feedback and testing results.
	+ Integration of AI-powered design tools and automation.

### **Weeks 9-12: Marketing and Launch Preparation**

* Objective: Prepare for launch and create a marketing strategy to reach target customers.
* Activities:
	+ Development of marketing materials, including website, social media, and email marketing.
	+ Creation of sales and customer support processes.
	+ Planning for launch events and webinars.
